LADOLA. Wear the Wow.

Life & Art · Detail & Originality · Look & Attitude

“Wear” is the act of dressing and expressing yourself. “Wow” is the sense of admiration, pleasure and confidence that a look can inspire.

Together, Wear the Wow. invites you to turn a chosen fabric into a personal expression of style.

At LADOLA, we believe art belongs in everyday life. We value original thinking, thoughtful design and attention to detail, creating fabrics that give you room to express your own style.

The wow may begin with a colour, a pattern or a texture that catches your eye. It comes to life through your choice of cut, the way you wear it and the confidence you bring.

Bringing your whole look to life through colour, detail and personal confidence.

Know it is LADOLA

Check the bale before you buy.

Brocade is widely copied. Look for these marks on every bale we ship.

  1. 1The LADOLA TapeA printed tape with "LADOLA" around the bale.
  2. 2The Barcode LabelA label with the barcode and the length of the piece, such as 30 metres.
  3. 3Sealed and Labelled PackagingClear sealed packing with the collection label on the front.
